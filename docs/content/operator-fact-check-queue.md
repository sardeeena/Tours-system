## Why this queue exists

The production catalogue contains broad or generic statements in booking-critical fields. These statements should not be rewritten as marketing copy until the operator confirms the actual rules for each activity.

The current snapshot shows:

- **25 of 26** tours say “Suitable for all ages. Children under 2 join free.” This is not a safe assumption for activities such as quad biking, horse riding, snorkelling, hiking or parasailing. Confirm child eligibility and any age/height/weight limits per operator.
- **1 of 26** (horse riding) has no age guidance.
- **23 of 26** use generic cancellation wording that asks customers to contact the booking team.
- **23 of 26** use generic pickup wording that defers approved locations and supplements to the booking flow.
- **2 of 26** claim pickup is included from all hotels within Sharm El Sheikh city.
- **1 of 26** (horse riding) lists specific included and additional transfer zones.

## Exact operator answers needed

For **each tour**, provide the following from the responsible operator or current supplier contract:

1. **Age and participation:** minimum age for passengers and drivers, child/infant eligibility, adult supervision rules, and any height, weight, swimming ability, fitness or health requirements. State “not applicable” only when confirmed.
2. **Cancellation and refunds:** exact free-cancellation deadline, refund percentages/deadlines, no-show rules, rescheduling options, and any supplier-specific exceptions. Confirm whether the displayed policy is local time and whether fees are deducted.
3. **Pickup:** included hotels/areas, excluded areas, supplement amounts or calculation method, meeting-point fallback, pickup time communication, and whether airport/port pickup differs from hotel pickup.

Also confirm activity-specific safety details for quad/ATV, horse riding, parasailing, banana boat, snorkelling/diving and the Mount Sinai hike. Do not use a generic “all ages” statement for these products without operator evidence.

## Publication rules

- Keep current live fields unchanged until each product's answers are approved.
- Do not infer missing restrictions from other operators or similar tours.
- Do not change pricing, booking availability, inclusions, departure times or refund logic as part of this copy review.
- After approval, update each tour as a complete product record and run booking, display, SEO and regression tests.
- Record who confirmed each fact and when, so the business can review it when supplier terms change.
